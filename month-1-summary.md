# Month 1 Summary

## Research Question
Which settlements in Ogbia L.G.A., Bayelsa State, Nigeria, falls within 200m of a watercourse and have low lying elevations?

## Operation Run and Why
- I used Buffer, Intersection, Spatial Join, and DEM-based elevation statistics 
- This was to examine settlement proximity to waterways, ward relationships, and elevation characteristics.

## Expected vs. Obtained
- I expected to identify settlements within 200 m of waterways and compare their elevation
- I obtained 629 of 1,066 settlements within 200 m of waterways alongside settlement-level elevation information.

## What Surprised Me
- Some settlements crossed ward boundaries 
- Areas farther from waterways had higher elevations than areas closer to waterways and this one is quite intuitive though

## Data Still Needed
- Future updates will incorporate population, rainfall, drainage, flood-event, and other relevant data for more comprehensive flood modelling.
