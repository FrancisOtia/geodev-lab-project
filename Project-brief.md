# My project Brief

## Part 1: The Question
Which settlements in Ogbia Local Government Area, Bayelsa State, Nigeria sit in low-lying land within 200 metres of a watercourse?

## Part 2: Why It Matters
Ogbia L.G.A. sits in the Niger Delta, where seasonal flooding and rising water levels regularly cut off or damage settlements near creeks and rivers. Local government emergency planners and NGOs working on flood response would use this to prioritise which communities need early-warning outreach, drainage investment, or evacuation planning ahead of the rainy season. It is also directly relevant to my own work on flood-risk mapping in the region. Worthy to note is that I, Francis Otia, The Author, am from Ogbia L.G.A. of Bayelsa State, Nigeria myself and I have witnessed several cases of flooding events first hand.

## Part 3: The Data I Need
- Ward boundaries for Ogbia L.G.A.
- Settlement extents/footprints for Ogbia L.G.A.
- Watercourses (rivers, creeks, streams) within Ogbia L.G.A.
- Elevation data covering Ogbia L.G.A.

## Part 5: What I Would Build
A map that flags settlements sitting on low-lying land within 200 metres of a watercourse, ranked by elevation relative to the nearest water body. This would be packaged as an interactive map that a local emergency planner or NGO officer could open before the rainy season to see, at a glance, which communities in Ogbia L.G.A. carry the highest flood exposure.
